# Verdure Materials — Content Pillars

Four pillars. Every LinkedIn post maps to one. They keep the feed varied and on-strategy, and they give the RAG system distinct retrieval territory (a "cost" brief should pull different chunks than a "material science" brief).

## 1. Evidence & Numbers — "show the data"

The flagship pillar. Embodied carbon figures, LCA boundaries, U-values, service-life data, sequestration math. Every claim anchored to a measurable. This is where Verdure earns credibility with technical specifiers.

- Example angles: kgCO₂e comparisons between materials; cradle-to-gate vs. cradle-to-grave; why "sustainable" needs a number.
- Tone: precise, neutral, confident.

## 2. Material Stories — "what it is and how it behaves"

Deep-dives on individual materials — hempcrete, mycelium panels, reclaimed timber, lime renders. How they're made, how they perform, their honest trade-offs (cure time, supply constraints, cost premium).

- Example angles: how mycelium is grown and heat-set; why hempcrete gets stronger as it carbonates; breathability and moisture strategy.
- Tone: craft-respecting, plain-spoken, never apologetic about bio-based aesthetics.

## 3. Honest Trade-offs — "credibility through candor"

The differentiator pillar. Verdure says no to greenwash, names cost premiums, admits supply limits, and talks clients out of unnecessary spends. This is where the brand's integrity becomes content.

- Example angles: turning down a veneer-greenwash project; reuse-first hierarchy; when NOT to specify a hero material.
- Tone: direct, principled, quietly confident.

## 4. Industry & Practice — "decarbonizing how we build"

The systems-level pillar. Whole-life carbon thinking, getting materials decisions upstream (RIBA stages), retrofit-first, policy and certification context.

- Example angles: embodied vs. operational carbon; why early-stage material decisions are cheap; retrofit before rebuild.
- Tone: optimistic but unsentimental, forward-looking.

## Cadence guidance

- Roughly 40% Pillar 1, 25% Pillar 2, 20% Pillar 3, 15% Pillar 4.
- Never two "Honest Trade-offs" posts back to back — it reads as performative humility. Space them.
- Every post: one idea, one pillar, a fact-or-tension hook, a quiet close.
