# Verdure Materials — Competitive Landscape

How Verdure positions against the alternatives a specifier is actually weighing. Useful context for the model when a brief touches comparison, objection-handling, or differentiation.

## The real competition isn't another bio-based brand — it's the default spec

Most projects don't choose between Verdure and a rival green supplier; they choose between Verdure and the conventional, high-carbon default (concrete block, mineral-wool, aluminum/uPVC cladding). The job of the content is usually to move embodied carbon onto the decision table at all, not to win a feature war.

## Competitor archetypes

### 1. "Conventional Co." — the incumbent default
- **Strengths:** cheap, familiar, abundant supply, contractors know it cold, zero program risk.
- **Weaknesses:** high embodied carbon, no credible end-of-life story, increasingly exposed as carbon regulation tightens.
- **Verdure's line:** we don't beat them on price or familiarity — we beat them on whole-life carbon and on being the spec that ages well as policy catches up. We compete by reframing the cost question around embodied carbon, not just upfront £/m².

### 2. "GreenWash Ltd." — the vague-eco supplier
- **Strengths:** great marketing, low-friction "sustainable" claims, easy yes for a client who wants a press release.
- **Weaknesses:** thin or absent LCA data, flattering cradle-to-gate-only numbers, products that don't survive technical scrutiny.
- **Verdure's line:** this is exactly who our **Honest Trade-offs** pillar is aimed at. We win by publishing both LCA boundaries, naming our trade-offs, and refusing greenwash projects. Candor is the moat. Never name-call — just out-evidence them.

### 3. "Spec Bio." — the credible bio-based peer
- **Strengths:** genuine, data-backed, similar values; a respectable competitor.
- **Weaknesses:** often narrower range (one material), or weaker on the upstream advisory / early-stage involvement.
- **Verdure's line:** we differentiate on breadth (hempcrete + mycelium + reclaimed timber under one spec), on getting involved at RIBA Stage 2, and on plain-spoken technical support that wins over skeptical contractors. Compete on rigor and partnership, never by disparaging a fellow traveler.

## Positioning summary

Verdure's defensible position = **evidence + candor + early involvement**. We are not the cheapest, not the loudest, and not the only bio-based option — we are the supplier whose numbers you can hand to an investor, whose trade-offs you already know, and who was in the room before the structural grid was locked.

## Tone rules when content touches competition

- Out-evidence, never trash-talk. Disparaging competitors reads as insecurity and violates the grounded voice.
- The hero is the carbon outcome and the reader's project — not Verdure beating a rival.
- It's fine to critique a *practice* (greenwashing, cradle-to-gate-only reporting) but not a named company.
